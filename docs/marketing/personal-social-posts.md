Every claim below is something the tool does today. Don't promise "send as a link" yet; it isn't built.

**What the tool does (for writing your own variations)**

- Free, no sign-up, no watermark
- Quotes and invoices with your logo, tax (VAT/GST) and notes
- Payment block: bank or mobile-money details, plus a payment link shown as a scannable QR code
- "Accepted? Make it an invoice": one click turns a quote into an invoice
- Download a PDF, or share on WhatsApp (on phones the PDF is attached)
- 40+ currencies, local date formats, works anywhere
- Drafts stay in the visitor's browser; we don't see their documents

## QR code

Opens `https://use-luminii.vercel.app/personal?utm_source=qr` (so QR visits can be told apart once sources are tracked). High error correction, navy on white, standard quiet zone. Tested with an independent decoder (jsQR).

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `personal-qr.svg` | Print: flyers, posters, business cards, stickers (scales to any size) |
| `personal-qr.png` | 1200 × 1200 px, for slides, documents and websites |
| `personal-qr-card.png` | 1080 × 1350 px ready-to-post card (Instagram/Facebook portrait, WhatsApp status) |

Print it at least **2 × 2 cm** (bigger for posters read from a distance), keep the white border, and don't place it on a busy background.

If the link changes (e.g. a custom domain), the QR codes must be regenerated.

## Posting tips

- **Show it working.** A 15–20 second screen recording of filling in an invoice and the PDF appearing beats any caption on Threads, Instagram and Facebook.
- **Groups beat pages early on.** Freelancer and side-hustle groups on Facebook, plus subreddits where self-promotion is allowed (e.g. r/freelance), give the most feedback for the least effort. Use the feedback-style post there.
- **Tag links by platform**, so you can later see which one works:
  - Threads: `use-luminii.vercel.app/personal?utm_source=threads`
  - Facebook: `?utm_source=facebook`
  - Instagram: `?utm_source=instagram`
  - LinkedIn: `?utm_source=linkedin`
  - X: `?utm_source=x`

  Note: the CRM dashboard doesn't record the source yet. It needs a small change to count visits and sign-ups by `utm_source`.
- **Where results show up:** CRM dashboard → "Personal builder (last 7 days)" (visitors, download, Send and email rates) and "Personal: what should we build first?" (votes).
